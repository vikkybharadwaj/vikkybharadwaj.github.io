My AI Mental Model

Customer in, outcomes out.

I run product as a loop, not a checklist: start from the customer, ship the smallest bet that moves a real outcome, measure it, and feed what I learn straight back into the roadmap. The AI craft below — pipelines, evals, the data flywheel — exists to serve that loop, not the other way around.

The AI PM operating loop. Explicit signal (what users say) and implicit signal (what users do) feed every turn — and each turn compounds: more usage → more signal → a better model → more usage. That return arc is the data flywheel.

Customer & business outcomes
🧭

Customer Discovery & Obsession

Start with the customer and work backwards. Continuous discovery — interviews plus real behavior — over a spec written once and frozen.

🎯

Outcomes Over Output

Roadmaps anchored to a North Star and business impact — activation, retention, conversion — not a feature checklist.

AI-native craft
🤖

AI Engineering & Agents

Claude & AWS Bedrock pipelines, agent tooling, prompt and structured-output design — turned into features users actually feel.

📐

AI Evals

Dimensions, fixtures, ground-truth, judge scoring. Evals are the new success metric — AI quality you can actually move.

🌀

The Data Flywheel

Every interaction is a signal. Corrections and usage feed back so the model gets sharper the more the product is used.
